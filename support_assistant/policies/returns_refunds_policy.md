Zepto Returns and Refunds Policy

Document Type: Customer Support Policy
Category: Returns, Refunds, and Order Issues

«Note: This is illustrative content for the capstone demonstration, not verified current Zepto policy. Replace example rules with official project-provided policies when available.»

1. Refund Eligibility

- Refund eligibility depends on the order issue, product category, and applicable policy.
- Customers should report missing, damaged, incorrect, or defective items through the official support channel.
- Customers may be asked to provide the order ID, item details, and photographs where relevant.
- The chatbot must not guarantee a refund before eligibility is verified.

2. Requesting a Refund

1. Open the order in the Zepto app.
2. Locate the relevant order or item.
3. Use the available help or support option to report the issue.
4. Provide the requested details.
5. Follow the instructions from customer support.

The exact steps may vary depending on the app version and issue type.

3. Refund Processing

- Refund processing time depends on the payment method and the status of the refund request.
- Customers should refer to the refund confirmation and payment provider for the applicable timeline.
- The chatbot must not invent a specific refund completion date.
- If a refund is delayed beyond the communicated timeline, the customer should contact support with the order ID and refund reference, if available.

4. Refund Methods

- Refunds are subject to the applicable payment and refund process.
- The available refund method may depend on the original payment method and the terms applicable to the order.
- The chatbot must not promise a refund to a particular bank account, wallet, or payment method unless confirmed by the system.

5. Cancellations and Refunds

- Refund eligibility for cancelled orders depends on the cancellation status and applicable terms.
- If payment was collected but the order was cancelled, customers should check the refund status in the app or contact support.
- The chatbot must not claim that a refund has been initiated without verified confirmation.

6. Non-Refundable or Restricted Items

- Some product categories may have specific return or refund restrictions for safety, hygiene, or regulatory reasons.
- Eligibility should be checked against the applicable product-specific policy.
- The chatbot must not declare an item refundable or non-refundable without supporting policy information.

7. Chatbot Guidelines

When answering refund questions:

1. Ask for the order ID if the issue is order-specific.
2. Explain the applicable policy using retrieved information.
3. Clearly distinguish between a refund request, an approved refund, and a completed refund.
4. Never fabricate refund status, amount, or processing dates.
5. Escalate unresolved or disputed cases to customer support.

End of Returns and Refunds Policy
