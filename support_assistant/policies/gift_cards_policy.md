Zepto Gift Cards Policy

Document Type: Customer Support Policy
Category: Gift Cards, Redemption, and Balance

«Note: This is illustrative content for the capstone demonstration, not verified current Zepto policy. Replace it with official project-provided policies when available.»

1. Gift Card Overview

- Gift cards, vouchers, or promotional codes may be subject to specific terms and eligibility conditions.
- Availability and supported gift card products should be checked through official Zepto channels.
- Customers should review the terms displayed at the time of purchase or redemption.

2. Gift Card Redemption

- Customers should follow the redemption instructions provided with the gift card or offer.
- A gift card or promotional code may have restrictions on eligible accounts, products, order values, or locations.
- The chatbot must not promise that a code will work without checking its applicable terms.
- Customers should ensure that the code is entered correctly and has not expired.

3. Expired or Invalid Gift Cards

- Gift cards and promotional codes may have an expiry date or other restrictions.
- Customers should verify the code, expiry date, and terms.
- If a valid-looking code does not work, customers should contact the official support channel with the relevant details.
- The chatbot must not claim that an expired code can be reactivated unless confirmed by an authorized policy or support system.

4. Gift Card Balance

- Customers should use the official app or the gift card provider's approved method to check any available balance.
- The chatbot must not invent a gift card balance or claim to have checked a balance without access to a verified system.
- Customers should contact support if a redemption or balance discrepancy remains unresolved.

5. Refunds and Transfers

- Refunds, transfers, resale, and cash redemption of gift cards depend on the applicable terms and law.
- The chatbot must not guarantee cash refunds, transfers, or replacement of gift cards without verified policy information.
- For purchase or redemption disputes, customers should contact customer support with the relevant transaction details.

6. Gift Card Security

- Customers should keep gift card codes and redemption details private.
- Customers should not share gift card codes, passwords, payment PINs, or one-time passwords with unauthorized parties.
- The chatbot should never ask customers to disclose passwords, payment PINs, or one-time passwords.

7. Chatbot Guidelines

1. Explain redemption instructions and restrictions using available policy information.
2. Do not invent balances, expiry dates, or code validity.
3. Refer unresolved redemption or payment issues to customer support.
4. Protect gift card and payment-related information.
5. Clearly state when account or transaction verification is required.

End of Gift Cards Policy
