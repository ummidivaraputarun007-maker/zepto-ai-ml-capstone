Zepto Order Cancellation Policy

Document Type: Customer Support Policy
Category: Order Cancellation and Cancellation Status

«Note: This is illustrative content for the capstone demonstration, not verified current Zepto policy. Replace it with official project-provided policies when available.»

1. Cancellation Eligibility

- Whether an order can be cancelled depends on its current status and applicable terms.
- Customers should open the order in the app and check whether a cancellation option is available.
- An order that has progressed to preparation or delivery may have different cancellation options.
- The chatbot must not guarantee cancellation without confirmation from the order system.

2. How to Request Cancellation

1. Open the Zepto app.
2. Navigate to the relevant order.
3. Check for an available cancellation or help option.
4. Follow the instructions displayed in the app.
5. Contact customer support if the cancellation option is unavailable.

The exact cancellation process may vary depending on the order status and app version.

3. Cancellation Confirmation

- Customers should check the order status after submitting a cancellation request.
- A cancellation request is not necessarily the same as a confirmed cancellation.
- The chatbot must not state that an order is cancelled unless the order system confirms it.
- If the order status is unclear, the customer should contact support with the order ID.

4. Cancellation and Refunds

- Refund eligibility depends on payment status, cancellation status, and applicable refund terms.
- If payment was collected, customers should check the refund status in the app or contact support.
- Refund processing times may vary by payment method.
- The chatbot must not invent refund amounts, completion dates, or confirmation numbers.

5. Cancellation After Dispatch

- Cancellation options may be restricted once an order has been dispatched or handed over for delivery.
- Customers should contact support if they need assistance with an order that is already out for delivery.
- The chatbot must not promise that a dispatched order can be stopped or recalled.

6. Chatbot Guidelines

1. Explain the general cancellation process using the available policy.
2. Ask for the order ID when an order-specific issue needs investigation.
3. Clearly distinguish a cancellation request from a confirmed cancellation.
4. Do not claim that a cancellation or refund has been processed without verification.
5. Refer unresolved cancellation requests to customer support.

End of Order Cancellation Policy
