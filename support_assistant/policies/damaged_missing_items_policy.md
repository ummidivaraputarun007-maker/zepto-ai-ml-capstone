Zepto Damaged and Missing Items Policy

Document Type: Customer Support Policy
Category: Damaged, Missing, Incorrect, and Defective Items

«Note: This is illustrative content for the capstone demonstration, not verified current Zepto policy. Replace it with official project-provided policies when available.»

1. Reporting Damaged Items

- Customers should inspect delivered items and report any damage or quality issue through the official support channel.
- Customers may be asked to provide the order ID, affected item name, description of the issue, and photographs where relevant.
- Customers should retain the affected item and packaging until support provides instructions, where practical.
- The chatbot must not guarantee a refund or replacement before the issue is reviewed.

2. Missing Items

- If an item is missing from an order, customers should report the issue through the app's help or support option.
- Customers should provide the order ID and identify the missing item.
- Support may review the order details and delivery information before deciding how to resolve the issue.
- The chatbot must not claim that an item was packed, delivered, or refunded unless verified information is available.

3. Incorrect Items

- Customers who receive an incorrect item should report the issue through the official support channel.
- Customers should identify the item ordered and the item received.
- Photographs or other details may be requested to help investigate the issue.
- The chatbot must not promise a replacement or refund without confirmation of eligibility.

4. Expired or Quality-Related Items

- Customers should report expired, spoiled, or otherwise defective products promptly through the official support channel.
- Customers should provide the product name, order ID, and relevant details such as expiry date or photographs when requested.
- Customers should avoid consuming products they believe may be unsafe.
- Product safety concerns should be referred to customer support for appropriate investigation.

5. Resolution Options

- Depending on the issue and applicable terms, possible resolutions may include a refund, replacement, or another support-approved remedy.
- The available resolution depends on the product, issue, order details, and applicable policy.
- The chatbot must not guarantee a particular resolution or compensation amount without verified approval.

6. Chatbot Guidelines

1. Acknowledge the reported issue politely.
2. Ask for the order ID and affected item details when needed.
3. Explain the reporting process using retrieved policy information.
4. Do not invent refund status, replacement status, or compensation amounts.
5. Escalate unresolved, disputed, or safety-related cases to customer support.

End of Damaged and Missing Items Policy
