1. Support Availability

- Customer support availability may vary by channel, location, and type of customer issue.
- Customers should check the official Zepto app or website for current support availability.
- The chatbot must not invent or guarantee specific support hours without verified information.
- If official support hours are not available in the knowledge base, the chatbot should clearly say that it cannot confirm the current schedule.

2. Contacting Customer Support

- Customers should use the Help or Support section in the official app or website.
- Customers should select the relevant order or issue category when available.
- Customers may need to provide their order ID and a description of the issue.
- Customers should not share passwords, payment PINs, or one-time passwords with support or the chatbot.

3. Escalation to Human Support

Issues that may require human support include:

- Refund or payment disputes.
- Orders marked delivered but not received.
- Missing, damaged, incorrect, or defective items.
- Cancellation requests that cannot be completed in the app.
- Account, membership, or gift card disputes.
- Safety-related or unresolved complaints.

The chatbot should explain why an issue requires further investigation and direct the customer to the official support channel.

4. Information Required for Support

Depending on the issue, support may request:

- Order ID.
- Affected product or item name.
- Description of the issue.
- Relevant photographs or transaction references, where appropriate.

The chatbot should request only information necessary to guide the customer and must not request sensitive authentication credentials.

5. Expected Response Times

- Response times may vary depending on issue type, support channel, and current demand.
- Customers should refer to any response-time estimate displayed by the official support channel.
- The chatbot must not promise that a human agent will respond within a specific period unless a verified policy or support system confirms it.

6. Chatbot Escalation Guidelines

1. Answer general policy questions using the retrieved knowledge base.
2. Identify when a question requires account-specific or live order information.
3. Clearly state when the chatbot cannot verify a customer's case.
4. Provide the official support route for unresolved or sensitive issues.
5. Never claim that a support ticket has been created unless the system confirms it.
