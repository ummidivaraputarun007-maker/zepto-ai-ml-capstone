Zepto Delivery Policy

Document Type: Customer Support Policy
Category: Delivery and Shipping
Purpose: Answer customer questions about delivery estimates, delays, and delivery charges.

«Note: This is illustrative policy content created for the AI/ML capstone demonstration. It is not a verified statement of Zepto's current commercial policies. Replace these example rules with official policy information if provided by your project.»

1. Delivery Time Estimates

- Delivery estimates depend on the customer's location, store availability, order size, and current demand.
- The estimated delivery time displayed during checkout is an estimate and may change after the order is placed.
- Customers should check the order tracking page or app for the latest available delivery estimate.
- During periods of high demand, severe weather, or operational disruption, deliveries may take longer than estimated.

2. Delivery Delays

- If an order is delayed, customers should first check the order tracking status.
- If the tracking information has not changed or the order is significantly delayed, customers should contact customer support.
- Support agents should verify the order status before providing an explanation or an updated delivery estimate.
- The chatbot must not invent a delivery time or claim that an order will arrive at a specific time without verified tracking information.

3. Delivery Charges

- Any applicable delivery charge should be displayed to the customer before the order is placed.
- Delivery charges may depend on the customer's location, order value, and applicable offers or membership benefits.
- Customers should refer to the checkout screen and their order receipt for the actual delivery charge.
- The chatbot must not promise free delivery unless the applicable offer or membership terms confirm eligibility.

4. Delivery Address

- Customers should verify their delivery address and contact details before placing an order.
- Address changes after an order is placed may depend on the order's preparation and delivery status.
- Customers should contact support promptly if they entered an incorrect address.
- The chatbot must not claim that an address has been changed unless the change is confirmed by the order system.

5. Undelivered Orders

- If an order is marked delivered but the customer has not received it, the customer should contact support for an order-status investigation.
- The customer should provide the order ID and registered contact details through the approved support channel.
- The chatbot must not state that a refund or replacement has been approved without confirmation from the order or support system.

6. Chatbot Response Guidelines

When answering delivery-related questions:

1. Use the delivery policy and retrieved information as the source of truth.
2. Ask for the order ID when order-specific information is required.
3. Do not invent tracking details, delivery estimates, fees, or compensation.
4. Explain when the answer depends on live order information unavailable to the chatbot.
5. Direct customers to the official support channel when a human investigation is required.

End of Delivery Policy
