Zepto Membership Tiers Policy

Document Type: Customer Support Policy
Category: Memberships, Benefits, and Eligibility

«Note: This is illustrative content for the capstone demonstration, not verified current Zepto membership policy. Replace these example rules with official project-provided policies when available.»

1. Membership Overview

- Membership plans may provide benefits such as delivery offers, discounts, or promotional access.
- Available plans, prices, and benefits depend on the current program and customer eligibility.
- Customers should refer to the membership section of the official app for current plan details.

2. Membership Benefits

- Benefits may vary by membership tier.
- Possible benefits include eligible delivery-fee discounts, special offers, or member promotions.
- Each benefit is subject to its displayed terms, eligible products, minimum order value, location, and validity period.
- The chatbot must not promise a discount or benefit unless the applicable terms confirm eligibility.

3. Membership Pricing and Renewal

- Membership prices and durations should be checked in the official app before purchase.
- Renewal terms, where applicable, should be disclosed during enrollment.
- Customers should review the displayed terms before confirming a membership purchase.
- The chatbot must not invent membership prices, renewal dates, or subscription charges.

4. Membership Cancellation

- Customers should use the membership or account settings available in the app to review cancellation options.
- Cancellation effects depend on the applicable membership terms.
- The chatbot must not claim that a membership has been cancelled unless the system confirms it.
- For billing disputes or unavailable cancellation options, customers should contact customer support.

5. Membership Eligibility

- Membership benefits may depend on the customer's account, location, order, and offer conditions.
- A benefit may not apply to every product or order.
- Customers should check the offer details and checkout screen to confirm eligibility.

6. Membership and Refunds

- Refund eligibility for membership purchases depends on the applicable terms and payment status.
- The chatbot must not guarantee a membership refund without verified policy information.
- Billing disputes should be referred to customer support for review.

7. Chatbot Guidelines

When answering membership questions:

1. Explain only benefits supported by the available policy.
2. Do not invent membership tiers, prices, savings, or renewal conditions.
3. Direct customers to the official app for current membership offers.
4. Distinguish general membership information from account-specific eligibility.
5. Escalate unresolved membership or billing issues to customer support.

End of Membership Tiers Policy
